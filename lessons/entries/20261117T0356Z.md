## What failed
My first draft of two hand-written assertions was convoluted (a stream-mapped coin expectation and an arithmetic-padded 292 constant); I rewrote them as plain literals before running. No test failures.

## Root cause
Writing expected values while thinking about the implementation led to over-clever assertions that could not clearly fail. Plain literal expectations are readable and auditable.

## Rule learned
For DP routines, pair a few literal known answers with randomized brute-force/reference cross-checks and structural validity checks on the reconstructed solution; write expectations as plain literals, never computed expressions that mirror the code.
