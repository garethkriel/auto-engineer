# Lesson 20261112T1615Z (pipeline test round)

## Task
Celsius/Fahrenheit converter with an absolute-zero guard (level 1).

## What worked
Testing known points in both directions with `@CsvSource`, plus a round-trip property test.

## What failed
Nothing failed. A first idea of comparing `f < -459.67` directly would have rejected the exact boundary because of floating-point rounding.

## Root cause
Converted boundary values are not exactly representable in binary floating point.

## Rule learned
When validating a boundary on a computed double, compare with a small epsilon (here 1e-9), and test the boundary value itself.
