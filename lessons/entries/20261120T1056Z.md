## Task
Level 20 capstone: `ae.chess`, a chess rules engine (strict FEN, make/unmake, legal move generation with castling, en passant and promotion, SAN, game status, alpha-beta and forced-mate search). Overlap check: no chess task in `benchmark/java/` (j01..j35) or `skills/java/`.

## What worked
- Published perft counts (six standard positions, depths up to 5) gave an objective oracle: all 30 cases passed on the first run, including colour-mirrored FENs, which would expose any white/black asymmetry.
- Deriving castling and en passant from the move shape (king moves two files; pawn moves diagonally onto the ep square) kept `Move` a plain record and `unmake` simple.
- Parsing SAN by matching against the canonical SAN of every legal move needed no separate grammar and cannot accept an ambiguous move.
- Mutation kill-checks: dropping the f1 attack check, the castle-out-of-check test, the rook-capture castling-right update and the knight promotion each failed 1-10 tests.

## What failed
Two test runs. Run 1: 4 failures and 2 errors, all wrong test fixtures, none in the engine:
- I expected `O-O-O+` but a rook on d1 does not check a king on e8.
- A "two rooks to d1" fixture had the white king on e1 between them, so only one rook could move.
- Two FENs were illegal: K v K ends the game at once, and a rook on b1 attacks a king on h1.
- Random playouts never reached an en passant capture (0 of 3000+ positions), so my "special moves exercised" assertion correctly failed.
A mutation check then found a survivor: dropping the "captured pawn really exists" test for en passant, which only a bogus FEN can trigger; I added a test for it.

## Root cause
Hand-written fixtures were not checked for legality or geometry (blockers, check, game already over) before being used, and uniform-random playouts almost never hit rare move kinds.

## Rule learned
- For board-game fixtures, name every blocker and attacker on the relevant line before writing the expected value, and make sure the fixture is a legal position that is not already game-over.
- Randomised playouts must bias towards rare move kinds (castling, en passant, promotion) and assert minimum counts of each; add a dedicated fixture for state that only malformed input can produce.
- Run mutation kill-checks even when every oracle passes: perft passing 30/30 still left one untested guard.
