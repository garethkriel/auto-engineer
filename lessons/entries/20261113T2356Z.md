## Task
Level 4 practice: loan amortization schedule (`BigDecimal` money, `java.time` due dates, `Optional` lookup, custom checked `InvalidLoanException`).

## What worked
Passed on attempt 1. Two design choices removed the usual failures: the final installment is computed as `balance + interest` (so rounding of the level payment can never leave a residual balance), and due dates are `firstPaymentDate.plusMonths(i)` from the original date rather than chained from the previous due date (chaining would turn Jan 31 -> Feb 28 -> Mar 28). Hand-checkable fixtures (10000 at 12% over 12 months: interest 100.00, payment 888.49) were derived before writing expectations, applying last round's rule.

## What failed
Nothing in the code or tests. Maven hit the transient "Could not find artifact" error twice (resolved by looping `mvn -U`; not counted as an attempt).

## Root cause
No failure to analyse. The transient Maven error is the known negative-cache issue already in `lessons/RULES.md`.

## Rule learned
When rounding each periodic amount, make the final period absorb the residual (derive it from the remaining balance) and assert invariants (principal parts sum to the loan, final balance is exactly zero, balances strictly fall) rather than only spot values; compute repeating calendar dates from the anchor date, never from the previous result.
