## Task
Level 20 capstone: `ae.datalog`, a Datalog engine (parser with positions, arity/safety/stratification checks, stratified negation, `!=`, naive and semi-naive evaluation). Overlap check against `benchmark/java/`: j35-minisql (SQL), j08-graph (search), j22-regex and j28-script (other languages) are not fixpoint logic engines.

## What worked
Keeping a NAIVE mode in the engine as the oracle for SEMI_NAIVE, plus an independent Floyd-Warshall oracle for transitive closure, gave two orthogonal checks. A mutant that limited delta variants to the first body atom was killed by both the random differential test and the fixed programs. Stratification by monotone relaxation with a `> predicate count` bound was short and detected negative cycles.

## What failed
Attempt 1: 24/25 passed; the random-program test threw "not stratifiable". Attempt 2: 25/25 passed. Also fixed before running: wrong hand-counted expectations (9 not 10 unreachable pairs; strata counts) found on re-read.

## Root cause
The random generator claimed "negate only a lower-index predicate => always stratified" but a lower predicate could depend positively on a higher one, closing a cycle through the negation. The analyzer was right; the generator's invariant was wrong.

## Rule learned
When a generator must produce only valid programs, state its validity invariant as a layering over the *whole* dependency relation (positive edges to index <= head, negative edges to index < head), not just over the one edge kind being constrained. Re-derive hand-counted expectations (pair counts, stratum counts) from the fixture before the first run.
