Level 3 (collections, Comparator): implement `Leaderboard` with ranking, competition ranks, top-n and score grouping.

## What worked
10 tests written first (ties, case-insensitive name tie-break with a deterministic final tie-break, overflow, unmodifiable views). First `mvn -q -B -U test` run passed: 10 tests, 0 failures; a plain re-run also passed.

## What failed
Nothing in the code. A `sed` on the pom template replaced every `<artifactId>`, including the JUnit dependency's; caught by diffing against the template and redone with a targeted `CHANGE_ME` replacement.

## Root cause
Over-broad sed pattern `<artifactId>[^<]*</artifactId>` matched all artifactId elements, not just the project's.

## Rule learned
When templating a pom, substitute the exact placeholder (`CHANGE_ME`) and `diff` against the template: the only changed line should be the project artifactId.
